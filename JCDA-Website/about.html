About JCDA

The Jamaica Community Development Association (JCDA) is a non-profit organization dedicated to improving communities through education, youth programmes, environmental projects, and public events.

Founded to encourage community participation, JCDA works with residents and volunteers to create positive change throughout Jamaica.

Our Vision

To create empowered and sustainable communities where every individual has opportunities to grow and succeed.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support community development through education, collaboration, environmental responsibility, and youth empowerment.

Our Values

  • Community Service
  • Integrity
  • Teamwork
  • Innovation
  • Sustainability

Our Achievements

  • Provided educational support programmes for youth.
  • Organized community clean-up projects.
  • Supported local development initiatives.
  • Hosted community awareness events.
